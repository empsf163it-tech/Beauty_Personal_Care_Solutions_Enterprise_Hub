OUR POINT OF VIEW

Less screen noise.
More room to shine.

BeautyOS is a concept for salons, spas, beauty clinics and independent professionals who want to spend less time juggling tools and more time caring for clients.

The visual identity pairs futuristic display lettering with a simple, readable interface: deep-space black, graphite grey, neon green, and a touch of cosmic violet.

THE FULL TOOLBOX

Everything a beauty team
needs, in one place.

Six everyday essentials designed to work together, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Online booking page

A shareable page where clients pick a service, a time and a stylist in seconds.

Gentle reminders

Friendly appointment reminders that help reduce missed visits and last-minute gaps.

Team & rota

Set shifts, assign services and see at a glance who is free, in or on a break.

Client notes

Preferences, patch tests and favourite treatments remembered for every visit.

Sales summaries

Recorded sales and your most-booked treatments, summarised without the clutter.

Clear permissions

Give owners, managers and stylists the right level of access to the right details.
